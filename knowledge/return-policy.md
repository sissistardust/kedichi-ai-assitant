# Return Policy

## Return Window

Customers may return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

## Return Conditions

Returned items must:

- Be unused
- Be in their original packaging
- Include all original accessories

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

- Opened pet food
- Personalized products
- Gift cards

## Refund Process

Once the returned item is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 5–10 business days.

Refunds are issued using the original payment method.

## Exchanges

Products may be exchanged if they arrive damaged or defective.

## Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.

## Contact

For return requests, please contact our customer support team before sending any item back.
