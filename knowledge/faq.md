# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## What products does Kedichi sell?

Kedichi offers pet products for cats and dogs, including food, treats, toys, grooming supplies, beds, accessories, and health products.

## Do you ship internationally?

Currently, Kedichi ships within the United States only.

## How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping usually takes between 3 and 7 business days.

## Can I track my order?

Yes. Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a tracking number by email.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal.

## Can I return a product?

Yes. Most products can be returned within 30 days of delivery, provided they are unused and in their original packaging.

## How can I contact customer support?

You can contact our support team by email or use our AI assistant, Dedè, for answers to common questions.

## Are your products safe for pets?

Yes. We carefully select products from trusted suppliers to ensure quality and safety.
